Title: Grade 3 Junior High_no.1

# Jobs

100: This person teaches students at school.
A: The answer is a teacher.

200: This person works in a hospital. They tell you why you are sick.
A: The answer is a doctor.

300: This person cooks food in a restaurant.
A: The answer is a chef.

400: This person puts out fires.
A: The answer is a firefighter.

500: This person writes stories and books.
A: The answer is a writer.

# Countries

100: Tokyo is the capital of this country.
A: The answer is Japan.

200: People in this country eat pizza and pasta. The capital is Rome.
A: The answer is Italy.

300: The Statue of Liberty is in this country.
A: The answer is the U.S.A.

400: Kangaroos live in this country.
A: The answer is Australia.

500: Big Ben is in the capital city of this country.
A: The answer is the U.K. (England).

# Animals

100: A baby dog is called this.
A: The answer is a puppy.

200: It is the biggest animal in the sea.
A: The answer is a whale.

300: It is a bird. It says 'hoot' at night.
A: The answer is an owl.

400: It is slow. It has a hard shell on its back. It lives in the sea or on land.
A: The answer is a turtle.

500: This small insect makes honey.
A: The answer is a bee.

# Daily Life

100: You sleep on this at night.
A: The answer is a bed.

200: You eat this meal in the morning.
A: The answer is breakfast.

300: You use this to clean your teeth.
A: The answer is a toothbrush.

400: You use this to take pictures.
A: The answer is a camera.

500: You use this to call or text your friends.
A: The answer is a phone.

# Takahagi

100: How many elementary and junior high schools are there in Takahagi?
A: The answer is seven. (four elementary + three junior high)

200: Takahagi is in this prefecture.
A: The answer is Ibaraki.

300: Takahagi is next to this big ocean. It is on the east side.
A: The answer is the Pacific Ocean.

400: This beautiful place in Takahagi is famous for red leaves in fall. It has a long bridge.
A: The answer is Hananuki Gorge.

500: A man from Takahagi made a famous map of Japan about 250 years ago. Who is he?
A: The answer is Nagakubo Sekisui.

# Space

final: This is the biggest planet in the solar system.
