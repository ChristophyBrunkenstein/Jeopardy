Title: Halloween - Grade 1 Junior High

# Monsters

100: It is white. It says "Boo!" You can see through it.
A: The answer is a ghost.

200: She wears a black hat. She flies on a broom.
A: The answer is a witch.

300: It is very old. It is wrapped in white cloth.
A: The answer is a mummy.

400 (DD): He comes out at night. He has long teeth. He drinks blood.
A: The answer is a vampire.

500: It has no skin. You can see the bones. It is white.
A: The answer is a skeleton.

# Pumpkins

100: What is "Pumpkin" in Japanese?
A: The answer is かぼちゃ.

200: A pumpkin is this color.
A: The answer is orange.

300: You cut a pumpkin and make a scary or funny face. You put a light inside. It is called this.
A: The answer is a jack-o'-lantern.

400: These are small and white. They are inside a pumpkin. You can eat them.
A: The answer is seeds.

500: It is a sweet food. It is round and flat. It is made from pumpkin.
A: The answer is pumpkin pie.

# Trick or ....

100: Children say, "Trick or ___!"
A: The answer is treat.

200: Children get this at Halloween. It is sweet. It comes in many colors.
A: The answer is candy.

300: What month is Halloween on?
A: The answer is October.

400: It is brown. It is sweet. Many people love it.
A: The answer is chocolate.

300: Children wear this on Halloween.
A: The answer is a costume.

# Costumes

100: A witch wears this on her head. It is black and pointed.
A: The answer is a hat.

200: A witch flies on this.
A: The answer is a broom.

300: A vampire wears this on his back. It is black and long.
A: The answer is a cape.

400: You wear this on your face. It hides your face.
A: The answer is a mask.

500: A ghost costume is often made from this. It is white. It is on a bed.
A: The answer is a sheet.

# Spooky Animals

100: It is black. It says "meow." It is a witch's friend.
A: The answer is a black cat.

200: It flies at night. It sleeps upside down.
A: The answer is a bat.

300: It has eight legs. It makes a web.
A: The answer is a spider.

400: It is a big bird. It says "hoo, hoo." It is awake at night.
A: The answer is an owl.

500: It is like a dog. It is wild. It howls at the moon.
A: The answer is a wolf.

# Halloween Night

final: It is round and yellow. It is in the sky at night.
A: The answer is the moon.
