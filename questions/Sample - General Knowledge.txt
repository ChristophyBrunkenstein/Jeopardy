Title: General Knowledge

# Space

100: This is the star at the center of our solar system.
A: The answer is the Sun.

200: This planet is famous for its beautiful rings.
A: The answer is Saturn.

300: Neil Armstrong was the first person to walk on this.
A: The answer is the Moon.

400: This is the name of the galaxy that contains our solar system.
A: The answer is the Milky Way.

500: This planet is the hottest in our solar system, even though Mercury is closer to the Sun.
A: The answer is Venus.

# Animals

100: This farm animal says “moo.”
A: The answer is a cow.

200: A baby kangaroo is called this.
A: The answer is a joey.

300: This is the largest animal on Earth.
A: The answer is the blue whale.

400: Bats, whales and humans all belong to this group of animals that feed their babies milk.
A: The answer is mammals.

500: A group of lions is called this.
A: The answer is a pride.

# Geography

100: There are this many continents on Earth.
A: The answer is seven.

200: Paris is the capital city of this country.
A: The answer is France.

300: This continent is the coldest on Earth, and is almost completely covered in ice.
A: The answer is Antarctica.

400: Mount Everest is part of this huge mountain range in Asia.
A: The answer is the Himalayas.

500: This is the capital city of Australia (hint: it's not Sydney).
A: The answer is Canberra.

# Word Wizard

100: This word is the opposite of “hot.”
A: The answer is cold.

200: A word that means the same as another word, like “big” and “large.”
A: The answer is a synonym.

300: This kind of word describes a noun, like “red” or “enormous.”
A: The answer is an adjective.

400: “There,” “their” and “they're” are examples of these: words that sound alike but are spelled differently.
A: The answer is homophones.

500: This punctuation mark shows ownership, as in “the dog's bone.”
A: The answer is an apostrophe.

# Math Mania

100: 7 + 5
A: The answer is 12.

200: 6 × 7
A: The answer is 42.

300: This shape has exactly three sides.
A: The answer is a triangle.

400: This is half of 250.
A: The answer is 125.

500: This is the next number in the pattern: 2, 4, 8, 16, …
A: The answer is 32.

# Our Planet

final: This is the only planet in our solar system that is not named after a god.
A: The answer is Earth.
