Title: Grade 1 Junior High_no.1

# Animals

100: It says 'meow.'
A: The answer is a cat.

200: It is big. It has a long nose.
A: The answer is an elephant.

300: It is black and white. It lives in a cold place. It cannot fly.
A: The answer is a penguin.

400: It is very tall. It has a long neck.
A: The answer is a giraffe.

500: It lives in Australia. It jumps. The baby lives in a pocket.
A: The answer is a kangaroo.

# Food

100: It is yellow. Monkeys like it.
A: The answer is a banana.

200: Japanese people eat this every day. It is white.
A: The answer is rice.

300: It is cold and sweet. You eat it in summer.
A: The answer is ice cream.

400: It is round. It is from Italy. It has cheese on it.
A: The answer is pizza.

500: It is a drink. It is made from oranges.
A: The answer is orange juice.

# Colors

100: The sky is this color.
A: The answer is blue.

200: A lemon is this color.
A: The answer is yellow.

300: Red and white make this color.
A: The answer is pink.

400: Black and white make this color.
A: The answer is gray.

500: Blue and yellow make this color.
A: The answer is green.

# School

100: You write with this. It is long and thin.
A: The answer is a pencil.

200: You read this. It has many pages.
A: The answer is a book.

300: You sit on this in class.
A: The answer is a chair.

400: You study English in this room.
A: The answer is a classroom.

500: It tells the time. It is on the wall.
A: The answer is a clock.

# Numbers

100: 1 + 1
A: The answer is two.

200: 5 + 5
A: The answer is ten.

300: 3 × 3
A: The answer is nine.

400: 20 − 8
A: The answer is twelve.

500: 10 + 10 + 10
A: The answer is thirty.
