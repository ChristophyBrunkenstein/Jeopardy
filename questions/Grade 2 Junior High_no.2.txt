# School Subjects

100: You learn about numbers in this class.
A: The answer is math.

200: You sing songs and play instruments in this class.
A: The answer is music.

300: You run and play sports in this class.
A: The answer is P.E.

400: You draw and paint in this class.
A: The answer is art.

500: You do experiments in this class.
A: The answer is science.

# Transportation

100: It has four wheels. You drive it on the road.
A: The answer is a car.

200: It flies in the sky. You take it to go to other countries.
A: The answer is an airplane.

300: It has two wheels. You pedal it.
A: The answer is a bicycle.

400: It is a very fast train in Japan.
A: The answer is the Shinkansen.

500: It floats on the sea. It carries people and cars.
A: The answer is a ship.

# Shapes

100: A ball has this shape. It is round.
A: The answer is a circle.

200: It has three sides.
A: The answer is a triangle.

300: It has four sides. All the sides are the same.
A: The answer is a square.

400: It has four sides. Two sides are long and two sides are short.
A: The answer is a rectangle.

500: It has five points. You can see many of them in the night sky.
A: The answer is a star.

# Days and Months

100: This day comes after Sunday.
A: The answer is Monday.

200: This is the first month of the year.
A: The answer is January.

300: This is the last month of the year.
A: The answer is December.

400: This day comes before Wednesday.
A: The answer is Tuesday.

500: Valentine's Day is in this month.
A: The answer is February.

# Nature

100: It is in the sky in the daytime. It is very hot and bright.
A: The answer is the sun.

200: You see it in the sky at night. It can be round or look like a banana.
A: The answer is the moon.

300: It is very tall. It has a trunk and many leaves.
A: The answer is a tree.

400: It is a big yellow flower. It turns to face the sun.
A: The answer is a sunflower.

500: Water flows in this. It is long and goes to the sea.
A: The answer is a river.

# Ocean

final: It is not a fish. It lives in the sea and it is very smart. It likes to jump.
