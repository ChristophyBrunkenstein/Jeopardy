Title: Grade 1 Junior High_no.2

# Body

100: You see with these.
A: The answer is eyes.

200: You hear with these.
A: The answer is ears.

300: You wear shoes on these.
A: The answer is feet.

400: It is in the middle of your face. You smell with it.
A: The answer is a nose.

500: You have ten of these on your hands.
A: The answer is fingers.

# Family

100: She is your father's wife.
A: The answer is your mother.

200: He is your mother's father.
A: The answer is your grandfather.

300: She is your mother's sister.
A: The answer is your aunt.

400: He is your mother's brother.
A: The answer is your uncle.

500: Your aunt's child is your this.
A: The answer is your cousin.

# Clothes

100: You wear this on your head. It keeps the sun off your face.
A: The answer is a hat.

200: You wear these on your feet.
A: The answer is shoes.

300: You wear these on your hands in winter.
A: The answer is gloves.

400: You wear this around your neck in winter.
A: The answer is a scarf.

500: You wear this coat when it rains.
A: The answer is a raincoat.

# Fruit

100: It is red and round. It grows on trees.
A: The answer is an apple.

200: It is small and red. It is sweet. People put it on cakes.
A: The answer is a strawberry.

300: It is round and orange. You peel it.
A: The answer is an orange.

400: They are small and purple. You eat many at one time.
A: The answer is grapes.

500: It is big and green outside. It is red inside. You eat it in summer.
A: The answer is a watermelon.

# Seasons

100: It is hot. You go swimming.
A: The answer is summer.

200: It is cold. It snows.
A: The answer is winter.

300: It is warm. Cherry blossoms open.
A: The answer is spring.

400: The leaves turn red and yellow.
A: The answer is fall (autumn).

500: In June there is a lot of rain in Japan. This season is called 'tsuyu.'
A: The answer is the rainy season.

# Animals

final: It has black and yellow stripes. It is a big cat.
A: The answer is a tiger.
