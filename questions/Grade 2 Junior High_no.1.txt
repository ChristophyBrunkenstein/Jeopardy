Title: Grade 2 Junior High_no.1

# Animals

100: It is a pet. It says 'woof.'
A: The answer is a dog.

200: It lives in the sea. It has eight arms.
A: The answer is an octopus.

300: It lives in Africa. It has black and white stripes.
A: The answer is a zebra.

400: It can fly at night. It is not a bird.
A: The answer is a bat.

500: It is the fastest animal on land. It has spots.
A: The answer is a cheetah.

# Food

100: It is red. It grows on trees. It is a fruit.
A: The answer is an apple.

200: You eat this for breakfast. It comes from a chicken.
A: The answer is an egg.

300: It is Japanese food. It is rice with raw fish.
A: The answer is sushi.

400: It is a vegetable. It is orange. Rabbits like it.
A: The answer is a carrot.

500: It is made from potatoes. It is long and thin. You can eat it at a hamburger shop.
A: The answer is French fries.

# Sports

100: You kick the ball in this sport.
A: The answer is soccer.

200: You hit a yellow ball with a racket.
A: The answer is tennis.

300: You do this sport in a pool.
A: The answer is swimming.

400: Shohei Ohtani plays this sport.
A: The answer is baseball.

500: Six players are on a team. They hit the ball over a net with their hands.
A: The answer is volleyball.

# Town

100: You buy food here. It is a big shop.
A: The answer is a supermarket.

200: Sick people go to this place.
A: The answer is a hospital.

300: You borrow books from this place.
A: The answer is a library.

400: Trains stop at this place.
A: The answer is a station.

500: You send letters from this place.
A: The answer is a post office.

# Weather

100: Water falls from the sky. You use an umbrella.
A: The answer is rain.

200: It is white and cold. You can make a snowman.
A: The answer is snow.

300: The sun is shining. There are no clouds.
A: The answer is sunny.

400: A very strong wind and heavy rain. It comes to Japan in summer and fall.
A: The answer is a typhoon.

500: This season comes after summer. The leaves change color.
A: The answer is fall (autumn).

# Japan

final: This is the tallest mountain in Japan.
A: The answer is Mt. Fuji.
